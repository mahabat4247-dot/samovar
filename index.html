Our Story

A Heritage of Warmth & Flavor

At Samovar, we celebrate the rich culture of Russian feasts. Named after the traditional brass vessel that gathers families and friends around warm tea and rich conversations, our restaurant brings the finest authentic flavors to your table with contemporary elegance.

From hand-rolled pelmeni to velvety borscht infused with aromatic herbs, every dish is prepared with passion, premium ingredients, and respect for tradition.
